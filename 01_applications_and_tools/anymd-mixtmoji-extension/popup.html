Mixtmoji FAQ

Q: How does Injection work?

A: Clicking "INJECT" executes a programmatic content script in your active tab, targeting the text caret of whichever text box or input field you are currently focusing on, pasting the mixtmoji instantly.

Q: What is the AnyMD Sync button?

A: It posts the baked mixtmoji directly to your local Express endpoint (Port 3050) or your custom synced local database file.
