AnyMDDB Acquisition & Feeds FAQ

How do Tag & Mood Subscriptions work?

You can follow specific genres (e.g. LitRPG, Danmei), tags (e.g. Time Loop), or moods (e.g. Cozy Fantasy, Wholesome). When you scan subscriptions, AnyMDDB checks NovelUpdates, Open Library subjects, and Kindle charts, automatically filtering out books already in your collection and creating clean wishlist entries.

What Repositories Are Searched?

  • Project Gutenberg: Free, legal public domain EPUB downloads with 1-click auto-acquisition.
  • Open Library & Internet Archive: Public digital lending, read links, and bibliographic catalog search.
  • OverDrive & Libby: Digital library borrowing deep links.
  • Anna's Archive: Multi-mirror shadow library query URLs.
