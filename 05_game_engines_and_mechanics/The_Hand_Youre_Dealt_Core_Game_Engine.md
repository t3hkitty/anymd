The NekoDealt Core Engine governs the life-simulation tabletop card game mechanics.
🎴 Initial Hand (Identity)
1. The Person: Your role in society.

2. The Personality: Multiplier for all other cards (e.g., Genius, Noble).

3. The Spouse: First King/Queen pulled.

4. The Home: First Ace pulled (Suit determines the home's blessing).

5. The Starting Asset: Initial resource.

🎨 Suit Semiotics
• Red (Surplus):

  • ♦️ Diamonds: Currency/Win Condition (Additive).

  • ❤️ Hearts: Happiness (Multiplier for Diamonds).

• Black (Deficit):

  • ♣️ Clubs: Burdens/Hardships (Divisor for total score).

  • ♠️ Spades: Tools/Labor (1:1 cancellation of matching Clubs).

🏛️ The Royal Court
• Decides changeable laws and global events.

• Tax: Removes Diamonds.

• Relief: Removes Clubs.

• Civilization Changes: Long-term rule shifts.

🎲 Solo Play
Use dice to act as the Court's voice for law petitions and decrees.



## Negative Jobs & Penalties
- **Thieves & Assassins:** These roles can target other players to steal assets or disrupt their hand.
- **The Backlash:** As a consequence of their actions, they must take on the **Black cards** (Burdens/Clubs or Labor/Spades) of the player they affected. This represents the "heat" or "guilt" of the crime dividing their own success.

## Social Mechanics
- **Hardship Benefit:** Pulling 3 Black cards in a row triggers a "Court Grant" (lowest available Red card).
- **Inheritance:** Spouse or House cards in the first five are "Legacy" cards; draw replacements to keep a full hand.
- **Education:** Acts as a specialized modifier for your Job card's abilities.



## The Royal Court: Relational Dynamics
* **Kings & Queens (Lovers):** These are always "Lover" cards.
    * **Family Lover:** If the suit matches your Current Spouse, they are a Family Lover. This triggers **exponential modifiers** (massive score swings, positive or negative).
    * **Impact & Cost:** They impact Spouse cards regardless of suit and carry a high cost (upkeep/drama) unless you are currently Divorced.
* **Jacks (Children or Side Pieces):** These represent the next generation or secondary relationships.
* **The Remarriage Cycle:** Once Divorced (Lawyer action), the very next King or Queen you pull from the deck automatically becomes your **New Spouse**.



## Family Expansion & Lodging
* **Jacks & Numerical Value:** Jacks represent children, but the **numerical value** of the card (or accompanying number cards) determines the number of children (e.g., a "3" modifier creates Triplets, a "2" creates Twins).
* **Extended Family:** Jacks of **non-matching suits** represent nieces, nephews, or illegitimate children. They still count toward your total family size.
* **The Lodging Fee:** Every child/extended family member requires a **Room card**. If you do not have enough Rooms in your Home (Ace) to house them, you must pay a recurring **Lodging Fee** penalty to your total score.
* **The Cost of Growth:** Each additional family member increases the base maintenance cost, regardless of suit.



## Death & The Discard Loop
* **The Death Card (8):** If an 8 is paired with a family member card (Jack, King, Queen) of the matching suit, that family member is moved to the **Discard Pile** immediately.
* **The Opportunity Cycle:** When the Discard Pile reaches **over 20 cards**, it is reshuffled back into the Draw Pile. This ensures that past events and people return as new opportunities or recurring narratives rather than being removed from the game permanently.
* **Jacks (The Narrative Wildcard):** Jacks represent any family-adjacent role the player chooses (Children, Side Pieces, In-Laws, etc.). The narrative is player-driven.
* **Family Costs:** Remember that any Jack added to the story still increases the maintenance/lodging pressure on your Home (Ace).
