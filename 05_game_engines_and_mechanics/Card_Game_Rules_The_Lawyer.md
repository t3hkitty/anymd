# Card Game Rules: The Lawyer

Lawyer Card Rules
The Lawyer is a specialized card used to change a player's relational state.

Actions

• Divorce: Use this card to remove a Spouse card from your hand or field. This is particularly useful for removing the Spouse of Clubs, which carries a persistent negative modifier.

Costs & Conditions

• Standard Fee: To activate the Divorce action, the player must pay a penalty of one Red Card (Heart or Diamond) to the Lawyer.

• Pro Bono: A Lawyer may choose to work for free. [Insert specific Pro Bono trigger or dice roll condition here]

Strategy

Removing a negative modifier improves your score density, though the loss of a Red Card represents a significant opportunity cost.


## Pro Bono & Sliding Scale
The Lawyer's fee is now a sliding scale: "You get what you pay for."

* **Full Payment (Red Card):** Clean break. The Spouse and their negative modifier are pruned/removed entirely.
* **Pro Bono / Reduced Fee:**
    - **The Recurring Ex:** The Spouse isn't pruned; they are shuffled back into the draw pile to reappear later.
    - **Alimony Shift:** The negative modifier isn't removed; it's halved but becomes a permanent "debt" on your Home card.
    - **Property Loss:** You successfully divorce, but you must discard your "Starting Asset" or "Home" card as part of the settlement.


    - **Room & Item Loss:** Along with Property Loss, you must specifically discard one 'Room' modification from your Home and one 'Item' card from your inventory. The Lawyer takes these as payment in kind.



* **The Lien Mechanic:** If you cannot pay the alimony or legal debt immediately, a **Lien** is placed on your Home. The Home card (and its Ace-based blessing) cannot be counted toward your final score until the debt is cleared. 
* **Clearing a Lien:** Must pay the equivalent of the debt in Diamonds or a Red Card to 'lift' the restriction before the game ends.



* **The Lien Token:** This is a physical status effect. Place a specialized token on top of the Home (Ace) card. As long as the token remains, the card's text and scoring value are "Shadowed" (inactive). The token can only be removed by the 'Clearing a Lien' action.



* **Physical Representation:** The token is a **Heavy Seal**. Its physical weight on the card emphasizes that the asset is frozen and legally bound until the debt is satisfied.



* **Accessibility:** Divorce can **only** be performed by a Lawyer. However, it does not require a "Divorce" card; the action is inherent to the Lawyer character card once played.
