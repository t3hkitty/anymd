# Card Game Rules Wolf  Therapist

Wolf in Sheep's Clothing
The Wolf can flip the suit of any card in the opening hand (first 5 or 7 cards) to make it Evil.

• Effect: A "Dead Uncle" becomes a "Murdered Uncle."

• Scoring: Each successful flip adds to the score.

• The Sheep: Even if cards are flipped to Evil, the Sheep still builds a Monument.

Therapist
The Therapist helps change the suit of any hand card.

• Condition: The player must have the corresponding card in their hand or discard pile.

• Mechanic: This action counts as Pruning, increasing the probability density of the deck.
