A decoupled, high-density React widget that combines a giant, luminous, "perfectly blinding" digital time tracker with 1-click emotional check-in prompts designed specifically for the **AnyMD** and **MyBlackBox** ecosystems [cite: 350].

## 🧠 Cognitive Grounding Philosophy
This clock directly honors two crucial somatic specifications:
1. **The Perfectly Blinding Date Layout:** Keeps your temporal orientation grounded, preventing you from sliding into hyperfocus time blindness [cite: 255].
2. **Preset Melodramatic Inflation Chips:** Over-exaggerating minor inconveniences or time checkpoints (e.g., *"It's only 10am? Are we already in hell?"*) acts as a safe, low-stakes cognitive release valve, preventing emotional catastrophizing and grounding your sensory systems [cite: 294, 322].

## 🎹 Keyboard Shortcuts & Controls
- `Escape`: Instantly dismiss the interactive clock FAQ or Changelog modals [cite: 615].

- **Manual Ingest:** Type your own daily micro-reaction and click **Ingest** to push it straight to your daily Markdown vault log files [cite: 1, 197].
